Pressure Transducers & Fluid Measurement Analysis
Experimental and analytical study focusing on pressure measurement instrumentation, transducer calibration techniques, and establishing accurate fluid zero reference points.
Key Objectives
Transducer Calibration: Evaluate the response, linearity, and sensitivity of pressure transducers against known reference standards.
Fluid Zero Analysis: Investigate system zero-shift phenomena, hydrostatic pressure effects, and calibration baselines in fluid circuits.
Data Acquisition & Error Analysis: Process sensor readings, calculate measurement uncertainties, and generate calibration curves.
Tools & Contents
Tools: Pressure Transducers, Data Acquisition Systems (DAQ), Instrumentation & Calibration software.
Files Included: Experimental data sheets, calibration calculation reports, and final technical analysis (PDF).
